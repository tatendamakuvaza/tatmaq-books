The opportunity

Generic accounting software makes every industry buy the same chart of accounts

A mine does not sell "services". A school does not invoice "goods". An insurer carries unearned premium reserves; a hospital bills medical aid schemes. Off-the-shelf systems force accountants to bend their industry into a generic template — then rebuild it every year.

The problem

One-size-fits-all ledgers

Global SaaS ships a US/India template. Local SMEs pay for features they don't use and re-key industry detail into spreadsheets.

The shift

Activate your sector

Tatmaq Books installs a sector-specific chart of accounts, billing items, cost categories, asset register and KPI in one click.

The result

Books that speak your trade

Ore stockpiles and royalties. Tuition and boarding. Trips and tolls. Premiums and claims. Reported in language your board understands.
